# Cancellation and rescheduling policy (effective 1 January 2026)

> Demonstration content for the fictional Slotly studio. This version replaces the 2024 policy.

## Free cancellation up to 24 hours before
You can cancel a confirmed booking free of charge up to 24 hours before the appointment start time. Cancel from "My bookings" or ask the Slotly assistant. Once cancelled, the time becomes available for other customers straight away.

## Late cancellations (less than 24 hours)
Inside the 24-hour window, bookings can no longer be cancelled online. Please contact the studio by email at hello@slotly.example. Late cancellations are charged 50% of the service price.

## No-shows
If you do not attend and did not cancel, the full service price is charged.

## Rescheduling
Rescheduling is treated as a cancellation plus a new booking. It is free when made at least 24 hours before the original appointment. Inside 24 hours the late cancellation charge applies.

## When we cancel
If Slotly has to cancel your appointment, for example because a practitioner is unwell, there is no charge. We will contact you to offer the earliest alternative time.

## Refunds
Payment is taken at the studio after the appointment, so there is normally nothing to refund. Any late-cancellation or no-show charge is invoiced by email.
