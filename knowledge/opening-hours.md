# Opening hours and location

> Demonstration content for the fictional Slotly studio.

## Studio opening hours
- Monday to Friday: 09:00–18:00
- Saturday: 10:00–14:00
- Sunday: closed
- National holidays: closed on Republic Day (26 January), Independence Day (15 August), Gandhi Jayanti (2 October) and Diwali

Individual services may run shorter hours than the studio itself; the booking page for each service shows its exact open times.

## Time zone
All appointment times are shown in Indian Standard Time (IST, UTC+5:30). India does not use daylight saving time, so times never shift during the year. If you are booking from outside India, check the time zone note on the booking page.
