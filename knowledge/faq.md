# Frequently asked questions

> Demonstration content for the fictional Slotly studio.

## What should I expect on my first visit?
Arrive 5–10 minutes early so we can go through a short health and consent form. Your practitioner will ask about your goals before starting.

## What should I wear for a massage or physio session?
Wear comfortable clothing. For massage you will be appropriately covered with towels at all times. For physio, shorts or loose sportswear make it easier to assess movement.

## Can I book for someone else?
Bookings are personal to the account holder. The person attending should create their own free account so they receive the confirmation and can manage the booking.

## Can children use Slotly services?
Customers must be 16 or older. Customers aged 16 or 17 need a parent or guardian present for massage and physio sessions.

## Which payment methods do you accept?
Card and contactless payments at the studio. We do not accept cash or cheques.

## Do you offer online or video sessions?
Strategy Consultation and Career Coaching can run by video call on request — mention it in the booking notes. Massage, meditation, nutrition and physio sessions take place in the studio.

## Do you sell gift vouchers?
Not at the moment.

## Is my information private?
Your bookings are visible only to you and to studio staff. The Slotly assistant never shares one customer's bookings with another.
