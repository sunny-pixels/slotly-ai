# Cancellation policy (2024)

> SUPERSEDED — kept for audit only. Replaced by the 2026 cancellation and rescheduling policy.

Bookings could be cancelled free of charge up to 12 hours before the appointment. Cancellations made less than 12 hours before the start time were charged the full service price. Rescheduling was allowed once per booking at no charge.
