# Booking rules

> Demonstration content for the fictional Slotly studio.

## How booking works
You can book any open time shown on a service's booking page or suggested by the Slotly assistant. A booking is confirmed only when you see a confirmation screen with a booking reference such as SLT-ABC234. All of your bookings are listed under "My bookings".

Each time slot can be held by one customer only. If two people try to book the same time at once, the first confirmed booking keeps the slot and the other person is asked to choose a different time.

## How far in advance you can book
Our calendar is published up to 14 days ahead. Online booking for a time closes 1 hour before the appointment starts. For a same-day appointment inside that hour, please contact the studio.

## Accounts
You need a free Slotly account to book, so that only you can view or cancel your bookings. Staff can see the bookings they manage, but other customers never see your details.

## Payment
Payment is taken at the studio after your appointment, by UPI, card or contactless. We do not take deposits for online bookings. The price you pay is the price shown when you booked, even if the listed price changes later.

## Arriving for your appointment
Please arrive 5–10 minutes early. If you arrive late, your session still ends at the scheduled time so the next customer is not delayed.

## Rescheduling
To move an appointment, cancel the existing booking and book a new time. This is free as long as you cancel at least 24 hours before the original start time — see the cancellation and rescheduling policy.
