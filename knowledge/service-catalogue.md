# Service catalogue

> Demonstration content. Slotly is a fictional studio; services, prices and practitioners are sample data.

Each service is delivered by its own dedicated practitioner, so every service has its own calendar. Prices are in Indian rupees (INR) and include GST. The price shown when you book is the price you pay.

## Strategy Consultation
A focused one-to-one session with a senior business strategist. Bring a challenge — launching a product, pricing, hiring your first team, or preparing for investment — and leave with a prioritized action plan. Suitable for founders, freelancers and small-business owners.
- Duration: 60 minutes
- Price: ₹3,500
- Available: Monday to Friday, 09:00–17:00

## Career Coaching
Practical coaching for career changes, promotions and job searches. Sessions can cover CV and LinkedIn reviews, interview practice, salary negotiation and planning your next move. Ideal if you feel stuck or are preparing for an important interview.
- Duration: 45 minutes
- Price: ₹2,000
- Available: Monday to Friday, 10:00–18:00

## Deep Tissue Massage
A firm-pressure massage that targets chronic muscle tension, knots and stiffness in the back, neck and shoulders. Recommended for people who sit at a desk all day, train regularly, or carry stress physically. Tell your therapist about any injuries before the session starts.
- Duration: 60 minutes
- Price: ₹2,500
- Available: Monday to Friday 09:00–18:00, and Saturday 10:00–14:00

## Guided Meditation
A calm, beginner-friendly session of guided breathing and mindfulness meditation. A good choice if you want to manage stress, anxiety or poor sleep, or simply want a quiet reset in the middle of the week. No experience is needed and you can sit on a chair or a cushion.
- Duration: 30 minutes
- Price: ₹500
- Available: Monday to Friday, 09:00–18:00

## Nutrition Assessment
A consultation with a registered nutritionist who reviews your current eating habits, energy levels and goals, then builds a realistic meal plan. Helpful for weight management, sports performance, digestive comfort or simply eating better. Please complete the short food diary we email you before your appointment.
- Duration: 50 minutes
- Price: ₹1,500
- Available: Tuesday and Thursday, 09:00–17:00

## Physio Check-in
A short physiotherapy assessment for aches, minor sports injuries, back pain or recovery after an injury. The physiotherapist assesses movement, gives hands-on treatment where appropriate and shows you exercises to continue at home. This is not an emergency service.
- Duration: 30 minutes
- Price: ₹900
- Available: Monday, Wednesday and Friday 09:00–18:00, and Saturday 10:00–14:00
